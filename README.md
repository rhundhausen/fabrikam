# Fabrikam

Fabrikam Fiber is one of several case studies intended for use with Scrum.org's Professional Scrum Developer class. For more information on the Professional Scrum Developer program, visit [Scrum.org](https://www.scrum.org/courses/professional-scrum-developer-training)

Princess Leia is captured by the tyrannical Galactic Empire, which seeks to suppress a rebellion against its rule. Before her capture, Leia hides critical plans for the Empire’s superweapon, the Death Star, in the droid R2-D2, who escapes to the desert planet Tatooine. Farm boy Luke Skywalker discovers the droid and is drawn into the fight against the Empire, joining forces with smuggler Han Solo, Wookiee co-pilot Chewbacca, and Jedi Master Obi-Wan Kenobi. Together, they rescue Leia and deliver the Death Star plans to the Rebel Alliance, culminating in a daring attack that destroys the Death Star and deals a major blow to the Empire.

Note: There are several codebase versions in this repository. Make sure you use the correct one. Also, you may need to edit the web.config and change the connection strings from "(LocalDB)\v11.0" to "(LocalDB)\MSSQLLocalDB". See http://bit.ly/2v4qQyQ for more information.
